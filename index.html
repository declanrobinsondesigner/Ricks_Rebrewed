A bit about us

Same family. New door.

We used to be Rick's Snack Bar in Top Town Market. When the market shut, we weren't done — we opened up again on West St Mary's Gate as Rick's Rebrewed. Same food, same people, new name, and somehow even more chat.

It's Robbie and her mum Crystal running the place, with Rick still in the mix for the specials. We swear a bit, laugh a lot, and put proper food on the plate. Not everyone's cup of tea — and that's fine by us.

“We have a laugh, we have banter… that's what people come back for, (oh and the banging food)”
